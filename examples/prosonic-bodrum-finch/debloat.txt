# Netflix and Prime buttons: MediaTek's hotkey service (com.mediatek.tv.service) receives both as global buttons and
# starts Netflix / Prime Video itself; with them disabled the start fails in the log ("result code=-91"/"-92").
# The launcher remaps them with app_keys "launch:com.netflix.ninja:<app>" and
# "launch:com.amazon.amazonvideo.livingroom:<app>", vendor_key_log on and key_filter off: any key-filtering helper
# kills the held-volume ramp here, and filtered volume keys never reach the helper, so consume_volume_keys cannot
# bring it back. Android 14 asks "Allow access to all device logs?" over Home once per boot; allow it.
